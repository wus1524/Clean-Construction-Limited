What Drives Us

Our Story & Purpose

Based in Lae Urban, Morobe Province, Clean Construction Limited was established to provide reliable, professional, and quality construction services throughout Papua New Guinea. We proudly serve government agencies, private companies, resource-sector organizations, churches, educational institutions, communities, and individual clients.

We provide a broad range of services, including building construction, road and civil works, renovations, fencing, property maintenance, drainage, earthworks, concrete works, and general contracting.

Our company is strictly committed to delivering projects according to client requirements. We maintain the highest standards of workmanship, workplace health and safety, environmental responsibility, and professional conduct.
